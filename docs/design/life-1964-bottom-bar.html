LIFE-1964: the bottom bar, three ways to draw it

Every option has the same four places: Rooms, Looks, Edit, Settings. Rooms is Home. It shows the rooms only, with the house switch at the top and no recent looks. Pick one bar, or say what to change.

  • Each place keeps where you were. Tap Looks, scroll, tap Rooms, tap Looks again: you are where you left it.
  • Edit opens on the last look you edited, with no room first (LIFE-1967).
  • Settings holds what has no home today: light software, schedules, add a light, the house list.
  • On the laptop the same four places sit in a left column, and Ctrl+K opens the jump sheet.
