# Design system audit

App or system: Illumigoon, the one app (branch `life-1885-one-app`), with the patch editor in `packages/patch-editor`
Date: 3 Nov 2026
Checklist version: 2 Nov 2026, 277 rows, plus ST-15 added on 3 Nov 2026 (278 rows)
Issues: LIFE-1936 (gap 3), LIFE-1935

Status is one of: Met, Partly, Missing, Out of scope. Evidence is a file path, token name or test. Out of scope needs a reason.

## What was audited

- **The app's tokens and primitives:** `src/design-system/design-system.css` (the `:root` block is the token set) and
  the components behind `src/design-system/index.ts`. Composites in `src/composites`, with `composites.css`.
- **The editor's style sheet:** `packages/patch-editor/src/styles.css`, and the inline styles in
  `packages/patch-editor/src/ui` and `src/app`.

**The editor has no token set of its own.** `styles.css` defines no custom properties. Its header says every token
comes from `design-system.css` through `:root`, and a search confirms it: it reads 36 of the app's tokens (`--text-1`
to `--text-5`, `--track`, `--wash`, `--wash-strong`, `--modulation*`, `--type-*`, `--tracking-label`, `--float-shadow`,
`--scrim` and others). What makes the editor look different is what it writes by hand: about 320 raw `px` values,
`border-radius: 2px` written 31 times, raw `z-index` values from 1 to 300, raw heights, and about 300 inline
`style={{ }}` objects in its TSX. So "the editor's set" in this audit means the editor's recipes and hand-written
geometry, not a second list of tokens.

Machine checks run for this audit, on 3 Nov 2026:

- `node scripts/lint/rules.mjs`: no problems.
- `npx depcruise src --config .dependency-cruiser.cjs`: no violations, 124 modules.
- A search for `var(--name)` across both style sheets found no token that `design-system.css` does not define.
- `docs/friction/report.json` (from `npm run friction`, axe with `wcag2a`, `wcag2aa`, `wcag22aa` at phone width):
  22 problems, including serious `color-contrast` failures on Home, Looks and Save scene, and `meta-viewport`
  on every screen. 59 of 666 controls are under 44 px.

Decisions were filled in by an agent on Ruan's instruction of 3 Nov 2026 (see `decisions.md`). A row that waits on a
decision uses that choice.

## Worst gaps, by area

Each area is one Linear issue under LIFE-1936. Row IDs are in brackets.

1. **Accessibility basics.** No focus ring anywhere: inputs set `outline: 0` and no `:focus-visible` style exists
   (GE-9, AC-4). Zoom is blocked by `user-scalable=no` in `index.html` (AC-6, TY-7). axe finds contrast failures
   (CO-9, AC-2). No live regions (AC-13). No reduced-motion support (AC-9, MO-11, TR-11).
2. **Text below the floor.** `--size-small` is 10.5px, under the 11 pt owner rule. Labels, eyebrows and most editor
   text use it on the laptop (TY-4). Font sizes are px, not rem (TY-5).
3. **Editor geometry outside the tokens.** The editor writes its own spacing, radii, heights and z-index in
   `styles.css` and inline styles. `lint:rules` checks its colour and type but not its spacing (SP-1, SP-2, GE-1,
   GE-7, GE-10, TL-3, TL-5).
4. **Missing token groups.** No z-index, breakpoint, duration, opacity or border-width tokens. One easing token.
   One radius (TO-2, GE-4, GE-7, MO-1, MO-2, LA-1).
5. **Duplicated components.** Two toasts, two sheets, two faders, two swatches, four selected marks, and two icon
   sets (CM-2, CM-4, BR-8).
6. **Duplicated information on screens.** Ten places show one state twice (ST-15). See the list under the States
   table.
7. **Status colours.** Only `--bad` exists. `--warn` is a neutral grey by the no-amber rule. No success or
   information roles, no on-colour tokens (CO-3, CO-5).
8. **Tap targets.** 59 controls under 44 px at phone width. Editor targets are 22 to 28 px on the laptop
   (AC-3, GE-11).
9. **Shell, URLs and keyboard.** No URL per screen, no deep links, no keyboard route check (NA-7, NA-17, LA-7).
10. **No kitchen-sink page and no component pages.** Nothing shows every primitive in every state (TL-2, ST-1, CM-7).
11. **Written rules.** No brand principles, voice, vocabulary or style rules (BR-1, BR-4, BR-5, BR-6).
