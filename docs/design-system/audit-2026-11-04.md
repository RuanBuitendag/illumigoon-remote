## What was audited

- **Tokens and primitives:** `src/design-system/design-system.css` (the `:root` block) and the components behind
  `src/design-system/index.ts`: keys, `Fader` with `FaderBand` and `FaderDot`, `Dialog`, `Swatch`, `Picked`, `Icon`
  and `IconFrame`, marks, fields, pad and LED strip.
- **Composites:** `src/composites` behind `src/composites/index.ts`, with `composites.css`: among them `Sheet` on
  `Dialog`, `Toast`, `ChoiceList` and `NamedSelectRow`.
- **Edit:** `packages/patch-editor/src/styles.css` and the inline styles in `packages/patch-editor/src/ui`. Edit now
  imports the app's two look doors (D50) and uses `Fader`, `Sheet`, `Toast`, `Swatch`, `Picked`, `Icon`, `PowerKey`.
- **The component page:** `?components`, from `src/domain/screens/component-page` (D68).

Since 3 Nov the app moved onto Edit's look (D44 to D67). What is left of a second look is in Edit: its own `Seg`,
`Tabs`, `.check`, `.tog`, `.pill`, native selects and knobs, and the heights and widths it still writes by hand.

## Worst gaps, by area

1. **Edit fails axe.** Contrast of `--text-5` text and the blend chip, three hex fields with no label (CO-9, AC-2,
   TL-8, FO-1, CM-9). The earlier runs never reached Edit.
2. **Edit at 320 px.** One row of Edit runs 16 px past the edge, so the page scrolls sideways (LA-7, AC-6).
3. **Edit's own controls.** `Seg` beside `OptionRow`, `.check`, `.tog`, `.pill`, native selects and knobs. They
   are not on the component page, not under `raw-control`, and the knobs take no keyboard (CM-4, ST-1, TL-5, NA-17,
   AC-8).
4. **Sizes lint does not see.** `height`, `width` and box-shadow edges are raw in Edit and composites (GE-10, GE-4,
   TO-2, DA-1).
5. **Deep links and Back.** Hash addresses exist, but no `appUrlOpen` and `replaceState` keeps Back off the
   system gesture (NA-7, NA-6).
6. **Reorder has no other route.** Drag is the only way to reorder rooms, lights and layers (AC-5, CM-10).
7. **Motion and overlays.** No enter or exit easing, no exit tokens, sheets appear with no motion and no
   transition table (MO-2, TR-1, TR-4, TR-7).
8. **Platform settings.** Nothing reacts to `prefers-contrast` or `forced-colors` (CO-7, CO-8).
9. **Device checks.** Type, Dynamic Type, safe areas, VoiceOver and frame rate wait for the iPhone (TY-8, TY-15,
   LA-3, AC-11, MO-13).
